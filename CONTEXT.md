# School

School is a setting for teaching, learning, and evaluating people, models, and agents through recorded episodes. Roles describe a participant's relationship to the work; they do not identify a permanent kind of participant.

## Participants and material

**World**:
A setting with state, rules, observations, available actions, and consequences within which a task has meaning.

**Subject**:
The material, concern, or capability an episode investigates or teaches.

**Participant**:
An identified person, model, or agent taking a role in an episode.
_Avoid_: Using provider, model name, and role as interchangeable identities.

**Learner**:
A participant whose understanding, behavior, or capability is being investigated or developed.

**Teacher**:
A participant that selects or supplies lessons and assistance based on the learner's observed needs.

**Evaluator**:
A participant or procedure that assesses an attempt against a declared criterion, with its evidence and limits retained.
_Avoid_: Treating teacher agreement as independent verification.

**Executor**:
The participant or mechanism that carries out an admitted action and reports its actual outcome.

## Teaching and observation

**Lesson**:
A bounded arrangement of material, observations, tasks, and feedback intended to develop or investigate a capability.

**Task**:
A requested outcome with conditions by which its completion can be assessed.

**Observation**:
Information available to a participant from a stated source and access.
_Avoid_: Assuming an observation contains the world's complete truth.

**Attempt**:
A learner's actual answer, prediction, action, or proposed change in response to a task.
_Avoid_: Treating a proposed action as an executed effect.

**Assistance**:
Teacher-supplied information or choices that may affect an attempt, including examples, hints, answers, and update instructions.

**Evidence**:
A retained observation or checked result used to support or challenge a claim within its stated scope.

**Qualification**:
An assessment of what a claim or candidate is supported to do under declared conditions.
_Avoid_: Equating a confidence judgment with authority or an occurrence.

**Confidence**:
A participant's stated uncertainty about a claim or answer.
_Avoid_: Treating confidence as measured correctness.

**Evaluation result**:
The outcome of applying a declared criterion to an attempt, with the evaluator's identity and evidence.

**Gradient**:
A derivative that specifies local sensitivity of an objective to variables. A grade or evaluation result may inform an objective, but is not itself its gradient.

## Change and continuation

**Edition**:
An identified version of a participant's effective model, program, or other consequential representation.

**Capability**:
An ability attributed to a learner edition on the basis of tasks and qualifying evidence.
_Avoid_: Inferring capability from lesson attendance, graph membership, or a single confident response.

**Learning episode**:
A bounded history connecting a starting learner, teaching and attempts, any intervention, evaluation, and continuing work.

**Learning change**:
A declared change intended to affect later capability, including context, memory, program, parameter, or representation changes. Its mechanism and measured consequences remain separate.

**Adoption**:
The decision that makes a qualified candidate the active edition for specified work.

**Continuum**:
The accounted-for continuation of identities, meanings, consequences, relationships, and unfinished responsibilities through change.

**Record**:
A retained account that connects sources, participants, observations, attempts, effects, decisions, and their order.
_Avoid_: Treating a record of a claim as proof that the claim is true.
